Frankenstein

Mary Shelley
“Beware; for I am fearless, and therefore powerful.”
Meeting: 20 September 2026, 6:30 pm
Location: The Reading Room, 12 High Street
Pages: 280

Victor Frankenstein creates life, then abandons it. Shelley’s novel follows the consequences of that refusal of responsibility, told through letters, confessions and a creature who learns to speak for himself.

Meeting notes

We opened the season with a stormy evening and a book that gave us plenty to argue about: ambition, responsibility and the creature’s loneliness.

The discussion moved from Shelley’s scientific imagination to the question of who is really the monster. Several readers were surprised by how much of the story belongs to the creature himself.

Topics discussed at the meeting:

  • Creation and responsibility
  • Who is the real monster?
