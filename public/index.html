Previous meetings:

  • October 2026 Pride and Prejudice Jane Austen
    Meeting notes

    Our first meeting was small but lively: five readers, plenty of tea and a surprisingly heated debate about first impressions.

    We talked about Austen’s irony, the economics of marriage and the pleasure of watching Elizabeth and Darcy misunderstand each other so perfectly. The question of whether the novel is romantic or quietly ruthless divided the room.

  • September 2026 Frankenstein Mary Shelley
    Meeting notes

    We opened the season with a stormy evening and a book that gave us plenty to argue about: ambition, responsibility and the creature’s loneliness.

    The discussion moved from Shelley’s scientific imagination to the question of who is really the monster. Several readers were surprised by how much of the story belongs to the creature himself.
